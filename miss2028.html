The 2026 UK eclipse was a remarkably deep partial eclipse — an important event in its own right, not just a preview of Sydney 2028.

Sydney will experience partial solar eclipses again, but very deep partial eclipses are much less common. That makes events like 2026 worth remembering alongside totality itself.
