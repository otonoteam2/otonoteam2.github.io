WHERE TO
STAY?

Sydney offers different neighbourhoods for different trips. These are areas to explore, not confirmed hotel bookings or guaranteed eclipse viewing locations.

🌉

Crown Towers Sydney

Sydney's ultimate "six-star" experience. The sculptured tower dominates the skyline, and its west- and north-facing villas offer private, floor-to-ceiling glass views of the eclipse over the water. Web Guide Highlight: Feature their private open-air cabanas by the infinity pool deck as the most exclusive, low-crowd VIP viewing location in the city.

EXPLORE →
🌉

Park Hyatt Sydney

Arguably the most expensive real estate in the city, sitting directly on the water between the Opera House and the Harbour Bridge. Advice: Book the Cove Suite or Rooftop Suite. These feature expansive private terraces where guests can view the eclipse right under the shadow of the Harbour Bridge without leaving their rooms.

EXPLORE →
🏙️

Four Seasons Hotel Sydney

Booking a 'Full Harbour View' room gives you a direct, private vantage point looking north over the water toward the eclipse path. Located right at the edge of The Rocks, it is a short, flat walk to major public viewing spots.

EXPLORE →
🌊

InterContinental Sydney

Positioned steps from the Royal Botanic Gardens. It features a spectacular rooftop lounge with panoramic northward sightlines over the harbour. Situated in a restored 19th-century Treasury building, making it a favorite choice for premium international eclipse tour operators.

EXPLORE →
🚆

Shangri La Sydney

Famous for having some of the highest and steepest views in the city. The upper-tier rooms and the iconic Blu Bar on 36 look straight out across the Harbour Bridge and Opera House. Elevated high on the hills of The Rocks, providing clear clearance over surrounding high-rises.

EXPLORE →
🌳

Pier One Sydney Harbour

This heritage hotel is built on a pier extending directly over the water, sitting right alongside the southern pylon of the Harbour Bridge. Unmatched if you plan to charter a viewing vessel or want to experience the eclipse directly from a private over-water balcony.

IMPORTANT BEFORE BOOKING

YOUR HOTEL IS NOT YOUR VIEWING PLAN.

A hotel room does not automatically provide an unobstructed view of the eclipse. Nearby buildings, terrain, access restrictions and weather may affect visibility.

Choose accommodation based on your travel needs, then separately identify an appropriate viewing location. Check cancellation policies and travel arrangements before booking.

PLAN AHEAD

FOUR THINGS
TO CONSIDER.

01 • Arrive early

Consider arriving before eclipse day so you have time to explore Sydney and prepare your viewing plans.

02 • Check transport

Plan how you will reach your viewing location. Allow extra time for possible event-related crowds.

03 • Watch the weather

Check reliable forecasts closer to 22 July 2028. Cloud cover can prevent a clear view even inside the path of totality.

04 • Prepare eye protection

Use proper solar eclipse viewers during the partial phases. Follow established eclipse-viewing safety guidance.
