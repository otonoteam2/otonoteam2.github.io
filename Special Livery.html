✈️ A Fleet Built for Special Liveries

Otono Airways' special livery collection spans almost every part of its fleet, from enormous superjumbos and ultra-long-haul aircraft to Dreamliners and narrowbody jets. Each aircraft offers a different canvas for Otono's distinctive red-and-purple identity.

The Airbus A380 superjumbo provides the largest canvas in the current fleet, while the Airbus A350 family combines long, modern fuselages with designs inspired by astronomy, nature and the night sky. The Boeing 777 family carries some of Otono's most dramatic special liveries, with the future 777-9 and 777-10 creating even more room for large-scale artwork.

Otono's Boeing 787 Dreamliners feature their own collection of nature and seasonal themes, while the A350-1000ULR represents the airline's ultra-long-haul network. Smaller Airbus narrowbodies bring special liveries to shorter routes, and selected aircraft from Otono Airways' past are preserved here as part of the Legend Fleet.
